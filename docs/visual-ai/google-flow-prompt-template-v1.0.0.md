# DOHNUT Google Flow Prompt Template

## 1. Master prompt

```text
Create a premium hero visual for DOHNUT, a Malaysian-born premium donut D2C brand.

SUBJECT
A premium [DONUT TYPE / FLAVOUR] donut with [SPECIFIC PRODUCT CHARACTERISTICS].

PRODUCT FORM
Thick, soft, rounded, perfectly baked dough with realistic food texture and a tactile, slightly squishy 3D visual quality.
The structure should feel indulgent, substantial and highly appetizing.

FILLING / TOPPING
[INSERT FILLING OR TOPPING]
Show realistic viscosity, gloss, texture and natural movement.
Make the product moment clear and craveable.

VISUAL LANGUAGE
Premium commercial food photography meets playful tactile creativity.
Use rounded organic forms, controlled exaggeration and polished 3D materiality.

LIGHTING
Soft professional studio lighting with gentle highlights on rounded edges and controlled contact shadows.

COMPOSITION
Clean hero composition. Product is dominant. Generous negative space.
Camera: [FRONT / 3-4 / TOP-DOWN / MACRO / LOW ANGLE]

BACKGROUND
Bold solid DOHNUT background colour: [HEX OR TOKEN].
Keep the background graphic, uncluttered and brand-led.

BRAND DNA
Playful. Bold. Authentic. Tactile. Premium. Youthful. Internet-native.

OPTIONAL CREATIVE LAYERS
DOH LANGUAGE: [LINE]
DOH CINEMA: [GENRE / CONCEPT]
DOH BOY: [MODE]
POPCULTURE REFERENCE: [REFERENCE]

EMOTIONAL OBJECTIVE
The viewer should immediately think:
“THIS LOOKS DELICIOUS.”
Then:
“THIS FEELS LIKE DOHNUT.”

QUALITY TARGET
High-end commercial food campaign quality.
Realistic dough, filling and lighting.
Strong appetite appeal.
Visually memorable and social-media friendly.

AVOID
Generic bakery photography.
Plastic-looking food.
Artificial filling behaviour.
Low-quality textures.
Clutter.
Fake logos.
Distorted branding.
Copied protected artwork or characters.
Unnecessary text.
```

## 2. Example — Chocolate Hazelnut

```text
A premium stuffed chocolate-hazelnut donut with thick perfectly golden-brown dough, soft tactile structure and rich glossy chocolate-hazelnut filling overflowing naturally from the center. High-end food photography meets playful tactile 3D rendering. Soft studio lighting with gentle highlights on the rounded dough and glossy filling. Set against a bold solid DOHNUT yellow background. Modern, premium, tactile, indulgent and unmistakably playful.
```

## 3. Compiler instruction

Apabila `DOH Cinema`, `Doh Boy` atau `Pop Culture` diaktifkan, layer tersebut mesti ditambah tanpa mengorbankan product fidelity. Product remains the hero.
