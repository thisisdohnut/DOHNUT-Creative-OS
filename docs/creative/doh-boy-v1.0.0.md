# Doh Boy™ Character Bible

## 1. Role

**Doh Boy™** ialah recurring character DOHNUT. Dia bukan sekadar mascot; dia ialah social personality yang boleh bercakap, react, judge, celebrate, create lore dan menjadi face of campaigns.

## 4. Voice rules

### Say

- pendek
- confident
- playful
- local when natural
- self-aware

### Avoid

- corporate jargon
- lecture tone
- forced slang
- jokes at vulnerable groups
- unexplained in-jokes

## 7. Character canon

Doh Boy mesti recognizable across illustration, packaging, stickers, social, motion dan AI-generated scenes. Pose, expression dan costume boleh berubah; personality tidak boleh berubah tanpa versioned canon update.
