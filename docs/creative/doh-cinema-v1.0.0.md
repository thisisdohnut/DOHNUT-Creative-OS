# DOH Cinema™

## 1. Concept

**DOH Cinema™** menjadikan product launch dan social content sebagai mini entertainment properties. Formula teras ialah familiar pop-culture pattern + DOH twist + original DOHNUT execution + product relevance.

## 2. Formats

- Movie poster parody
- Trailer-style Reel
- Character scene
- Fake streaming thumbnail
- Episodic social series
- Limited-drop release poster

## 7. Production rule

Parody the **concept**, not the protected execution. Do not reproduce original movie logos, exact poster composition, characters, costume designs, studio marks or artwork. Build an original DOHNUT world.

## 8. Example

Input: launch Chocolate Hazelnut stuffed donut.

Concept:

> **DOH WICK — THE LAST BITE**
>
> A cinematic action campaign where the hero protects the final stuffed donut.

Why effective: entertainment device directly resolves into the product moment.
