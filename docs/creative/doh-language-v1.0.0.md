# DOH Language™

## 1. Definition

**DOH Language™** ialah sistem verbal DOHNUT yang memanipulasi perkataan **DOH**, **NUT**, **DOHNUT**, dan reaksi harian menjadi copy yang cepat difahami, cheeky dan shareable.

## 2. Core rules

1. Utamakan recognition dalam 2 saat.
2. Joke mesti membantu brand, bukan menenggelamkan product.
3. Kekalkan tone seperti kawan yang yakin, bukan corporation.
4. Pendek lebih baik daripada panjang.
5. BM + English boleh dicampur apabila natural untuk konteks Malaysia.

## 6. Few-shot examples

### Example A

Input: Launch stuffed chocolate hazelnut donut.

Output:

> **DOH NUT WAIT.**
> Chocolate Hazelnut is here.

Why effective: phrase is immediately recognisable and linked to a real product action.

### Example B

Input: Customer says one donut is enough.

Output:

> **DOH NUT LIE.**

Why effective: short, quotable and characterful.

### Example C

Input: Weekend campaign.

Output:

> **GET YOUR DOH ON.**
> Weekend tastes better with six.

Why effective: combines lifestyle emotion with product quantity.

## 7. Forbidden patterns

- Forced puns that are unreadable.
- Humour targeting protected/sensitive groups.
- Profanity that changes brand safety level without campaign approval.
- Copy that claims endorsement by another brand.
- Copy that obscures mandatory product information.
