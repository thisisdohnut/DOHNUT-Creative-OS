# DOHNUT Brand Constitution

## 1. Brand definition

**DOHNUT** ialah Malaysian-born premium donut D2C brand di bawah **GangNiaga Sdn. Bhd.**, berasaskan KL/Selangor (Lembah Klang), dengan model **online-first** dan **delivery-led**.

## 2. Positioning

> **The Playful Malaysian Donut Brand.**

DOHNUT menggabungkan premium donut quality + playful tactile creativity + modern Malaysian culture + digital-first experience.

## 3. Core product

- Classic
- Sprinkled
- Stuffed
- Specialty
- Signature / limited drops

## 4. Brand essence

### GOOD VIBE
Emotion: fun, happy, excited, connected.

### GOOD DOH
Product: good dough, good flavour, good craft, good quality.

### DOHNUT
Experience: product + emotion + culture.

## 5. Brand personality

| Trait | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Playful | bermain dengan idea, bentuk dan bahasa |
| Bold | tidak takut stand out |
| Cheeky | confident humour |
| Authentic | local, human, tidak terlalu corporate |
| Creative | eksperimen dan cultural remix |
| Social | content yang mengundang conversation |
| Premium | execution berkualiti tinggi |

## 6. Visual direction

- Bold, rounded, colourful and tactile.
- Premium commercial food photography.
- Playful 3D materiality.
- Soft/squishy dough cues.
- Original DOHNUT colour world.
- Clean composition with occasional controlled chaos.

## 7. Verbal direction

Suara DOHNUT ialah **kawan yang confident, cheeky dan culturally aware**. Campuran BM + English dibenarkan apabila natural.

## 8. Non-negotiables

1. Gunakan tagline rasmi **GOOD VIBE. GOOD DOH.** tanpa perubahan pada master tagline.
2. Jangan mengubah master logo tanpa approval.
3. Jangan reka fakta produk, claim nutrisi atau regulatory statement.
4. Jangan gunakan parody sebagai seolah-olah endorsement atau affiliation rasmi.
5. Jangan meniru asset visual protected secara dekat.
6. Product mesti kekal appetizing dan recognisable.

## 9. Audience

Primary: Gen Z dan young millennials sekitar 18-35, digitally native, menghargai taste, visual, convenience, culture dan shareability.

## 10. Brand promise

> Setiap interaksi dengan DOHNUT sepatutnya meninggalkan **better vibe** daripada sebelumnya.
